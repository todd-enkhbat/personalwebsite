# User identity and website positioning

Todd (Tsogt Enkhbat) founded Nomos Orbital, a **space infrastructure company**. This is the primary company and founder framing. Do not describe Todd as doing mission planning, or reduce Nomos to a mission planner. Existing software capabilities may be described accurately as supporting details without replacing the space infrastructure company positioning.

Project descriptions should explain the project itself, rather than lead with “I designed” or similar first-person accomplishment statements. Keep individual roles in role labels.

The homepage photo intro transitions automatically on entry, once per session. Do not add a visible enter/skip button. Respect reduced-motion settings.
