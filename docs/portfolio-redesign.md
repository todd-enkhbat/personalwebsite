## Evidence policy

Searches were conducted in English and Mongolian for each entry. Japanese institutional records were also checked for the balloon summit. Search results for similarly named organizations are not evidence of a relationship. Public organizational context and supplied personal contributions are separate evidence classes. Existing claims are retained without upgrading their verification status. No new awards, funding, university affiliations, customers, AI capabilities, or partnerships were inferred.

Original marks are used where available. Text fallbacks explicitly identified by `wordmark` are organization names, not reproductions of an authenticated visual identity. In particular, no logo has been invented for Helios, Starry Tours, or the unresolved Kublai identity. Relationship labels are exposed through alt text and tooltips. No institution is presented as an endorsement.

## Implementation and performance

One reusable `PortfolioCard`, one shared `OrganizationMarks`, and the retained `ProjectDossier` serve all entries. Grid uses four desktop / two tablet / one mobile columns with one 4:3 media system. Expanded dossiers preserve all original chapters, media and interactions, add references and 3D studies, and retain standalone routes.

Card objects are transparent offline-rendered thumbnails, lazy loaded except initial hero images. No card mounts a WebGL renderer. Interaction uses CSS transforms; reduced motion, touch devices, and slow-update devices use static previews. The expanded viewer loads dynamically and initializes on intersection. Geometry, renderer and observers are cleaned up on unmount. Existing cover photography remains a graceful WebGL fallback.

Rebuild models with `node --experimental-strip-types scripts/export-portfolio-models.ts`. Render previews with `PLAYWRIGHT_MODULE=<installed playwright path> CHROME_EXECUTABLE=<browser path> node scripts/render-portfolio-previews.mjs`.
