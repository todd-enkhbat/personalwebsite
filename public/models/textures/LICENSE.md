# Photographic model surface sources

Poly Haven photographic PBR material scans, CC0: https://polyhaven.com/license

- Fabric Pattern 07: https://polyhaven.com/a/fabric_pattern_07
- Metal Plate: https://polyhaven.com/a/metal_plate

Original 1K OpenGL normal and roughness JPEGs were downloaded from Poly Haven's public asset API. The `*-512.jpg` derivatives are resized to 512px for the interactive viewer and offline previews. Original object colors remain material parameters.

Jupiter cylindrical cloud map, NASA/JPL/Space Science Institute:
https://science.nasa.gov/photojournal/cassinis-best-maps-of-jupiter-cylindrical-map/
Original: https://assets.science.nasa.gov/content/dam/science/psd/photojournal/pia/pia07/pia07782/PIA07782.jpg

The Jupiter map is illustrative surface detail in the exoplanet study, not an observation of K2-18b or a new research result. This distinction is also stated in the project caption.

The Golden Record face, HUR artwork and MICC identity use existing supplied project assets. They remain illustrative presentation elements.
