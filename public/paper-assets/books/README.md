# Book cover sources

The 35 books in the lower shelf use the verified edition artwork in `curated/`. The current mapping is in `index.json`; exact ISBNs, binding, publisher, image dimensions, selected-edition links, proportions and rendering finishes are in `app/data/bookEditions.json`.

The October 14, 2026 audit selected 20 hardcover editions and 15 paperbacks, preserving the correct works and distinguishing collected editions and abridgments. Images were optimized to WebP at their native aspect ratios, with a maximum height of 1800 pixels and no enlargement. Three original sources remain modest: Deliverance from Error (318 × 500), City of Ladies (430 × 648), and Second Treatise (328 × 500). Their native size is preserved.

The complete per-book provenance is recorded in `cover-audit.json`. Some flat jackets use matching publisher-supplied digital artwork; the selected physical editions were separately verified. The earlier cover assets and historical `manifest.json` remain for reference.

The four featured books use:

- `dispossessed-featured.jpg`: [Ursula K. Le Guin’s official site](https://www.ursulakleguin.com/novels-and-collections), 50th anniversary edition.
- `nomos-publisher.jpg`: [Telos Press](https://www.telospress.com/store/-p17898060), original publisher artwork.
- `human-condition-featured.jpg`: [University of Chicago Press](https://press.uchicago.edu/ucp/books/book/chicago/H/bo29137972.html), 60th anniversary edition.
- `time-and-chance-featured.jpg`: [ThriftBooks](https://www.thriftbooks.com/w/time-and-chance_david-z-albert/1782431/), illustrated Harvard University Press edition.

The top header’s original transparent `ink-mark.png` is used for the personal foil/deboss treatment on the book backs and endpapers. Back-cover treatments are personal library designs, rather than reproductions of the publishers’ back covers.
